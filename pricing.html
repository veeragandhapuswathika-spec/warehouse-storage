NEED MORE INFORMATION?

Pricing Questions, Answered

Review these common questions before enquiring about a storage plan.

Contact Our Team
How much does storage cost?

Pricing depends on the space required, rental duration, storage conditions and any additional services. Contact StoreVault for a quote based on your specific requirements.

Are the three plans fixed packages?

No. Small, medium and large are indicative categories to help you begin comparing options. Confirm actual dimensions, availability and terms with our team.

Can I store business inventory?

You can enquire about inventory, packaged goods, equipment and supplies. Accepted items, handling arrangements and available space must be confirmed before making a booking.

Are there extra charges?

Ask for a complete breakdown of applicable charges, which may include deposits, handling, special storage conditions or other services. Confirm all terms before committing.

How do I request a storage quote?

Visit our Contact page and tell us what you need to store, the approximate space required and your preferred rental duration. Our team can discuss the next steps.
