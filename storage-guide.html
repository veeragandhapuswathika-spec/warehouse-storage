CHOOSE YOUR STORAGE TYPE

Every Item Deserves a Plan

Different belongings need different approaches. Explore these tips before deciding how to organize and store your items.

GUIDE 01

Furniture & Home Items

Clean furniture before storing it, disassemble larger pieces when practical, and protect surfaces with suitable covers.

Helpful tip: Keep screws and small fittings in labeled bags with the relevant furniture.
GUIDE 02

Business Inventory

Group stock by category, label cartons clearly and maintain an inventory list to make locating products easier.

Helpful tip: Use consistent labels and record quantities before moving items into storage.
GUIDE 03

Boxes & Seasonal Items

Pack similar items together, label boxes on multiple sides and place frequently needed items where they are easy to reach.

Helpful tip: Avoid making boxes too heavy, and use sturdy cartons suitable for their contents.
PLAN YOUR SPACE

How Much Storage Space Do You Need?

Start by listing what you intend to store. The right space depends on the size and quantity of your items, how they can be arranged, and whether you need regular access to them.

  • ✓ Make an inventory. List large furniture, boxes, equipment and other belongings.
  • ✓ Measure larger items. Check dimensions before selecting a storage space.
  • ✓ Plan the arrangement. Allow for safe stacking and access where needed.
  • ✓ Check facility restrictions. Confirm accepted items, access arrangements and available unit sizes.

STORAGE QUESTIONS

Things to Know Before You Store

Find answers to common storage planning questions. Confirm specific arrangements with your chosen facility.

How do I choose the right storage size?
Make an inventory, measure larger items and estimate the number of boxes you need to store. Ask the facility to help you compare available unit sizes before booking.
What should I do before storing furniture?
Clean and dry furniture, protect delicate surfaces, remove loose parts where practical and label any hardware you take off. Follow the facility's preparation and storage requirements.
Can I store business inventory?
That depends on the facility's permitted uses and storage conditions. Ask about inventory types, loading arrangements, access hours and any restrictions that apply to your products.
Which items may not be suitable for storage?
Facilities commonly restrict hazardous materials, flammable substances, perishable goods and illegal items. Always review the facility's prohibited-items policy before packing.
How can I check the cost of storage?
Review the available pricing information and ask about unit size, rental duration, deposits, additional charges and any applicable access fees before agreeing to a rental.
