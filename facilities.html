FACILITY QUESTIONS

Things to Know Before You Store

Have questions about choosing storage? Here are a few useful starting points.

Ask Us a Question →
What items can I store?

Accepted items depend on the facility and its storage rules. Tell us what you plan to store so we can discuss suitability and any restrictions. Hazardous, illegal or otherwise prohibited goods may not be accepted.

How do I estimate the space I need?

Make a list of your items and estimate their dimensions, quantity and stacking requirements. Our team can discuss your needs and help you explore suitable space options.

Can businesses enquire about inventory storage?

Yes. Businesses can contact us to discuss their inventory, packaged goods, supplies and equipment. Available space, handling arrangements and service terms should be confirmed before booking.

Can I ask about access and security?

Absolutely. Ask about facility access hours, entry procedures, available security measures and any requirements that apply to your storage arrangement.

How can I get pricing for a facility?

Contact our team with the type and approximate quantity of items, your preferred rental duration and any access requirements. We can then discuss the applicable options and pricing.
